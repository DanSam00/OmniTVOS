AirPlay and Chromecast

Send what's playing to an Apple TV, an AirPlay TV or a Chromecast, and control it from the Mac.

AirPlay

  1. While a video is playing, press A, or choose the AirPlay button on the control bar.
  2. Choose an Apple TV or AirPlay TV from the list. The video moves to it.
  3. To bring it back, open the list again and choose this Mac.
Video goes across for streams Omni plays with Apple's player, which is most of them. A few formats are played by Omni's own decoder; for those only the sound can be sent over AirPlay.

Using the AirPlay list from the keyboard

macOS draws the AirPlay list itself, and it doesn't respond to the arrow keys. To choose a device without the mouse, turn on System Settings ▸ Keyboard ▸ Keyboard navigation. Then Tab moves through the list and Space chooses a device. Esc closes the list.

Subtitles

The subtitle you've chosen goes to the TV with the picture, and changing it while AirPlaying changes it on the TV. Subtitles built into the stream go across. A subtitle added later from an add-on shows only on the Mac.

TVs that refuse the sound

Some AirPlay TVs (several LG and Samsung models) can't play Dolby Digital Plus sound. If a TV refuses the stream, Omni tries once more with the sound converted to a format the TV accepts. If the TV still refuses, the video comes back to the Mac and Omni tells you.

Chromecast

  1. While a video is playing, press C, or choose the Cast button on the control bar.
  2. Choose your Chromecast, or a TV with Chromecast built in. The first time, macOS asks to let Omni find devices on your local network. Allow it.
  3. The Chromecast plays the stream itself, and the Mac becomes a remote: play/pause, back and forward, and a timeline. Use ← → and Return, or click.
  4. Choose Stop Casting to stop. The Mac picks up where the TV got to.
The Chromecast fetches the stream directly, so it has to be able to reach it. Streams that only exist on this Mac (local files, some debrid proxies) can't be cast; Omni tells you when that's the case. Speakers and soundbars with Chromecast built in play the sound only.

No devices in the list?

  • Check the Mac and the Chromecast are on the same network.
  • Check System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network allows Omni.
  • Give it a few seconds; devices appear as they answer.
