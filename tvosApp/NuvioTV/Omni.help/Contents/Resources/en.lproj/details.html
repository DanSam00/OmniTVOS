Show and movie pages

Play, choose a source, pick an episode, watch the trailer and save to your library.

Playing

Play (or Resume) starts the best available stream. With Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Smart Playback ▸ Auto Select Stream on, Omni picks one for you; otherwise choose from the sources list.

Choosing a source

The sources list shows every stream your add-ons found, with its resolution, size, codec, audio and add-on. Use the filter chips at the top (← → on the chip row) to narrow by add-on or quality, then ↑ ↓ and Return to play one.

Series

Choose a season, then an episode. Episodes you've watched are marked; ones you haven't can have their artwork blurred to avoid spoilers (Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Blur Unwatched Artwork).

More on the page

  • Trailer: plays the title's trailer.
  • Add to library: saves the title to your library. It then reads In library; choose it again to remove the title.
  • Cast, crew, production companies, networks, collections and More Like This appear further down when TMDB is set up (see TMDB).

Press Esc to go back. The menu (☰, top-left) is available here too: press ← at the left edge.
