Troubleshooting

Fixes for no streams, buffering, missing Continue Watching, casting and sign-in problems.

No streams for a title

  • Install at least one stream add-on (Add-ons) and check its configuration; many need a debrid service linked.
  • Turn off Playback ▸ Smart Playback ▸ Cached Only if it's on.
  • Brand-new or obscure titles may simply have no sources yet.

A stream keeps buffering

Omni switches to a smaller source by itself after repeated long stalls. To choose one yourself, press S and pick a lower resolution or a smaller file. A larger Playback ▸ Buffer Profile can help on unsteady connections.

Continue Watching is empty, or differs between devices

  • Check you're signed in to the same Nuvio account and profile on each device.
  • Check each device uses the same Continue Watching source (Nuvio sync, Trakt or Simkl) in Settings ▸ Integrations, and is connected to it.
  • Use Sync Now on the Trakt or Simkl page to refresh.

Can't find a Chromecast or AirPlay device

  • Make sure both are on the same network.
  • Allow Omni in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network.
  • Some streams play only from this Mac and can't be cast. See AirPlay and Chromecast.

"Your session expired"

Choose Settings ▸ Account & Profiles ▸ Reconnect Account and sign in again.

Keys don't seem to do anything

Click once inside the Omni window so it's the active app, then try again. Press Esc to close anything that may be open. In the player, ? shows the keys.

Something else

Omni keeps a log at ~/Library/Logs/Omni/omni.log, which helps when reporting a problem. As a last resort, Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Reset Settings restores the defaults without touching your account.
