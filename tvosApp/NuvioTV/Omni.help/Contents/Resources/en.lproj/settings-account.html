Account & Profiles settings

Your Nuvio account, sync options and signing in or out.

Open Settings ▸ Account & Profiles.

OptionWhat it does
Sync Watched StateKeeps what you've watched in step with your Nuvio account across devices.
iCloud Settings SyncCopies your settings to your other Apple devices through iCloud. Choose Sync Now in Advanced ▸ iCloud Sync to push them immediately.
Reconnect AccountAppears when your session has expired. Sign in again to resume syncing.
Sign Out / Sign InLeave your account on this Mac, or sign in to one.

Profile options are covered in Profiles.
