Integrations

Connect Trakt, Simkl, Stremio, TMDB, MDBList, AI subtitles, debrid services and your own media servers.

All of these are in Settings ▸ Integrations.

Trakt and Simkl

Trakt and Simkl keep a record of what you watch (scrobbling), and either can be the source of your Continue Watching row.

  1. Choose Trakt or Simkl Account.
  2. Trakt: scan the QR code with your phone, or open trakt.tv/activate and enter the code shown.
  3. Simkl: scan the QR code, or open simkl.com/pin and enter the PIN shown.

Once connected, choose which service drives Continue Watching and what to sync. Sync Now refreshes from the service immediately. From Simkl's page you can also transfer your existing watch history, library and progress to Simkl.

Seeing different Continue Watching on different devices? Make sure each device is connected to the same service and uses the same Continue Watching source.

Stremio

Sign in with your Stremio email and password, then choose Import Library to bring over your library and watch history, or Sync From Stremio to refresh it. Importing only reads from Stremio; nothing is written back. Signing out keeps the imported titles.

TMDB

TMDB adds richer details: better artwork, cast and crew, production companies, networks, season posters, collections and More Like This.

  1. Create a free API key at themoviedb.org/settings/api.
  2. Choose Metadata Providers ▸ TMDB, turn on Enable TMDB and paste the key.
  3. Choose which parts to use under Modules, and the language under Localization.

MDBList ratings

Shows ratings from IMDb, TMDB, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, Trakt, Letterboxd and audience scores. Get a free key at mdblist.com/preferences, then choose Metadata Providers ▸ MDBList, turn on Enable MDBList Ratings, paste the key and pick the providers to show.

AI subtitles

Translates subtitles into your language when none exist. Choose AI Subtitle Translation, pick a provider (Google Gemini or OpenRouter) and paste its API key, then turn on Enable AI Translation and choose Translate To. In the player, the translation is in the subtitle settings.

Your own media: SMB shares and Jellyfin

  • SMB: choose SMB ▸ Add Server, enter the address, user name and password, then pick shares and Start Scan. Found films and shows join your library.
  • Jellyfin: choose Jellyfin ▸ Add Server, enter the server address and sign in, then choose the libraries to sync.
