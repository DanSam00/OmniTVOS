Getting around with the keyboard

Everything in Omni works from the keyboard as well as the pointer.

The basics

KeyWhat it does
← → ↑ ↓Move the highlight
ReturnOpen or choose what's highlighted
EscGo back one step: close a panel, leave a page, leave the player
← at the left edgeOpen the menu

The menu

The menu sits in the top-left corner. While closed it shows the current page's icon; on a show or movie page it shows ☰.

  • Press ← at the left edge of any page to open it.
  • Use ↑ ↓ to choose, Return to go there, → or Esc to close it.
  • The top item is your profile. Choosing it switches profiles.

The same pages are in the View menu. While a video is playing, the Player menu lists every player key.

Using the pointer

You can also click anything. The window can be resized or made full screen with fn F; Omni scales its pages to fit.
