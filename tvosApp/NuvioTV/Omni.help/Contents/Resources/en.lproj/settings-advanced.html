Advanced and About

Navigation options, diagnostics, iCloud sync and resetting settings.

Advanced

  • Navigation: Fast Horizontal Navigation and Smooth Bring Into View change how rows move.
  • Diagnostics: Playback Issue Reports, the Playback Debug Overlay (stream details while watching) and the Focus Highlighter.
  • iCloud Sync ▸ Sync Now: pushes your settings to your other devices now.
  • Reset ▸ Reset Settings: puts every setting back to its default. Your account, add-ons and library are kept.
