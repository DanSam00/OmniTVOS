Search and Discover

Find a show or movie by name, or browse by type, catalog and genre.

Search

  1. Open Search (⌘2).
  2. Type a title. Results appear as you type, grouped into movies and series.
  3. Press ↓ to move from the search field into the results, and Return to open one.

Your recent searches are listed under the field; choose one to run it again.

Discover

With nothing typed, Search shows Discover: a grid of titles from your add-ons. Use the filters above it to narrow it down:

  • Type: Movies or Series.
  • Catalog: which add-on catalog to browse, such as Popular.
  • Genre: one genre, or All Genres.

Highlight a filter and press Return to open its list, then ↑ ↓ and Return to choose.

Whether Discover lives in Search or elsewhere is set by Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Discovery ▸ Discover Entry.
