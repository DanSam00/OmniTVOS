Profiles

Add, edit and switch profiles, set a PIN and choose an avatar.

Switch profile

Open the menu (← at the left edge) and choose your profile at the top. You're taken back to Who's watching?.

Add a profile

  1. On Who's watching?, move to Add Profile and press Return.
  2. Name: press Return on the name field and type. ↓ moves on.
  3. PIN (optional): choose Set PIN and type 4 digits with the number keys, then type them again to confirm.
  4. Avatar: choose a category, then an avatar from the grid. Or choose Choose from Photos… for your own picture, or Image Link… to use a picture from the web.
  5. Choose Save. Esc leaves without saving.

Edit a profile

In Settings ▸ Account & Profiles ▸ Profiles: change the Profile name, Choose Avatar, and turn the Profile PIN lock on or off.

  • Remember Last Profile: open straight into the profile you last used.
  • Choose Profile on Return: ask who's watching again when you come back to Omni.

Delete a profile

On Who's watching?, right-click the profile and choose Delete Profile. This removes it from your Nuvio account on every device. The primary profile can't be deleted.
