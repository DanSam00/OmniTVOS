Playback settings

Player engine, auto-play, smart stream selection, audio and subtitle languages, buffering and stream badges.

Open Settings ▸ Playback.

Player

OptionWhat it does
Player EngineWhich player plays video. The default suits almost everything.
External PlayerOpen streams in another app instead. Forward Subtitles Externally passes subtitles along.
Auto-Play Next EpisodePlays the next episode after a countdown.
Post-Play RecommendationsSuggests something to watch next when a title ends.
Frame Rate MatchingSwitches the display to the video's frame rate where supported.
Buffer ProfileHow much to load ahead. Larger buffers help on slow or unsteady connections.

Player Buttons

Choose whether Picture in Picture, Episodes and Streams & Sources appear on the control bar.

Smart Playback

OptionWhat it does
Auto Select StreamPicks a stream for you when you press Play.
Use Top ResultTakes the first stream in the list.
Stream QualityThe resolution to aim for.
Match Subtitle LanguagePrefers streams with subtitles in your language.
Cached OnlyShows only streams your debrid service already has.
Prefer Hardware Decoded StreamsPrefers formats the Mac decodes in hardware.
Stream SortHow the sources list is ordered.

Audio & Subtitles

Preferred Audio and Preferred Subtitle set the languages chosen automatically. Forced Subtitles shows foreign-language parts only. ASS/SSA Override controls styled subtitles.

Stream Badges

Show file sizes, choose where badges sit, show the add-on's logo, or install the gold badge pack.
