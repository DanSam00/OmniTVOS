Continue Watching, the featured carousel, catalog rows and how to arrange them.

Continue Watching

The featured carousel at the top shows what you were watching, newest first, along with the next episode of shows you're following (Up Next). Move ← → along it and press Return to resume.

  • Remove a title: highlight it and press R, or right-click it and choose Remove from Continue Watching.
  • Start again from the beginning: right-click the title and choose Start from Beginning.
  • More info: right-click and choose More Info to open its page.

Where Continue Watching comes from (Nuvio sync, Trakt or Simkl) is set in Settings ▸ Integrations. See Integrations.

Catalog rows

Each row is a catalog from one of your add-ons, or a collection of folders. Move with the arrow keys and press Return to open a title or folder. The backdrop and details at the top follow whatever is highlighted, and a trailer plays behind it if trailers are on.

Arrange your rows

  • Reorder, hide or delete rows: Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Home Catalogs. Each row has buttons to move it up or down, to the top or bottom, to show or hide it, and to delete it.
  • Add a row back, or add a new add-on's catalog: Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Add Catalogs. Catalogs are grouped by add-on; open a group and choose the catalog. New add-on catalogs wait here instead of appearing on Home by themselves.
  • Collections (rows of folders such as Sports) are managed in Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Collections.

Layouts

Choose a layout in Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Home Layout ▸ Layout:

  • Modern: large rows with the highlighted title's backdrop behind them.
  • Compact: smaller rows, more on screen.
  • Grid View: each catalog as a grid, with a hero slideshow at the top made from the catalogs you pick under Hero Catalogs.

See Layout & Discovery settings for the hero, carousel, frosted glass and trailer options.
