The player

Every key, the panels for episodes, sources and settings, and what happens when a stream keeps buffering.

Keys

Press ? while watching to see this list on screen. The Player menu in the menu bar lists the same actions.

With the trackpad, swipe left or right on the video to scrub. To leave the player with the mouse, move it to bring up the controls and click × at the top right; it is also there while a stream is loading.

Panels

  • Episodes: jump to another episode of the series.
  • Sources: switch to another stream without leaving the player. The filter chips at the top work as on the show page.
  • Settings (,): Subtitles (track, delay, size and style), Audio (track, delay, boost) and Speed. Move between tabs with ← →.

Which buttons appear on the control bar is set in Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Player Buttons.

Skipping and the next episode

  • When an intro, recap or credits segment is known, a Skip button appears. Press ↑ to reach it and Return to skip.
  • Near the end of an episode a Next Episode card appears. With Auto-Play Next Episode on it counts down and plays the next one; choose Cancel to stay.
  • At the end of a movie, Post-Play Recommendations suggests something else, with its trailer.

When a stream keeps buffering

If a stream stalls for a long time repeatedly, Omni switches to another source by itself. It first tries one at the same resolution that's at least 1 GB smaller, then lower resolutions, each smaller than the last. If every option fails, it tells you so. You can always pick a source yourself with S.

Picture in Picture

Choose the Picture in Picture button (or Player ▸ Picture in Picture) to keep watching in a small window while you use other apps.
