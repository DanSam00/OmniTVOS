Layout & Discovery settings

Home layout, hero and carousel, frosted glass, trailers, catalogs, collections and discovery options.

Open Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery.

Home Layout

OptionWhat it does
LayoutModern, Compact or Grid View. See Home.
Hero SectionShows artwork and details for the highlighted title above the rows.
Featured CarouselShows Continue Watching as a large carousel at the top.
Hero CatalogsGrid View only: which catalogs feed the hero slideshow.
Auto-Scroll CarouselMoves the carousel on by itself. Off by default; a playing trailer always finishes first.
Blur Unwatched ArtworkBlurs episode stills you haven't seen.
Fullscreen Hero BackdropLets the backdrop fill the whole screen.
Calendar ViewList or Month.
Poster LabelsShows titles under posters.
Catalog Add-on NamesShows which add-on each row comes from.
Frosted Glass Behind RowsBlurs the backdrop behind the rows.
Liquid Glass StyleUses the menu's glass look for that pane, with one row on screen at a time.

Focused Poster

Expand Focused Poster to Backdrop widens a poster into its landscape artwork after you rest on it; Backdrop Expand Delay sets how long that takes.

Trailers

Autoplay Trailers plays a trailer behind Home for the highlighted title. Trailer Preview Sound turns its sound on, and Trailer Delay (0–10 s) sets how long to wait first.

Discovery

OptionWhat it does
Search StyleThe look of the Search page. On the Mac, Search always uses the keyboard-friendly classic layout.
Discover EntryWhere Discover appears.
Sort OrderHow Continue Watching is ordered.
Up Next From Furthest EpisodeSuggests the episode after the furthest one you've watched.
Show Unaired Next UpIncludes next episodes that haven't aired yet.
Hide Unreleased ContentLeaves out titles that aren't out yet.
Show Full Release DatesShows complete dates instead of just the year.
