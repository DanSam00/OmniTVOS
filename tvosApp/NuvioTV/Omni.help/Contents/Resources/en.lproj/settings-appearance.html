Appearance and Subtitle Style

Accent colour, card style, background, language, and how subtitles look.

Appearance

Open Settings ▸ Appearance.

  • Card & Poster Style: corner radius and Liquid Glass cards.
  • Focus Outline: the highlight colour.
  • App Background: the background colour; AMOLED Mode makes it pure black, and AMOLED Surfaces does the same for panels.
  • Language: App Language for Omni's own text.

Subtitle Style

Open Settings ▸ Subtitle Style for text size, bold, letter spacing and opacity, vertical position and margins, outline, and background colour and opacity. Reset Defaults puts them back. Size and delay can also be changed while watching, with ,.
