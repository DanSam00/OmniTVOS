Getting started

Sign in, pick a profile and get your first title playing.

Sign in to your Nuvio account

Omni uses your Nuvio account to sync your add-ons, library, watch progress, profiles and most settings across your Mac, Apple TV and iPhone.

  1. On the Sign in screen choose QR Code or Email.
  2. QR Code: scan the code with your phone's camera and approve the sign-in there. The Mac finishes signing in on its own. Choose Refresh QR if the code expires.
  3. Email: enter your Nuvio email and password, then choose Sign In.

To use Omni without an account, choose Continue without account. You can sign in later from Settings ▸ Account & Profiles ▸ Sign In.

Don't have an account yet? Create one on the Nuvio website, then sign in here. Everything you set up on another Nuvio device appears once you're signed in.

Choose a profile

After signing in you're asked Who's watching?. Pick your profile with the arrow keys and Return, or click it. Each profile has its own Continue Watching, library and settings.

To add someone, choose Add Profile. See Profiles for PINs, avatars and switching.

Install add-ons

Catalogs and streams come from add-ons. If your account already has add-ons, they load automatically. Otherwise add them from Settings ▸ Integrations ▸ Add-ons. See Add-ons.

Play something

  1. On Home, move to a title and press Return to open its page.
  2. Choose Play, or open Sources to pick a particular stream.
  3. While watching, press ? to see every key. See The player.
