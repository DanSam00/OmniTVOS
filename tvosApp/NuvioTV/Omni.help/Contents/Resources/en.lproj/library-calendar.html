Library and Calendar

Your saved titles, and the calendar of upcoming episodes and releases.

Library

Everything you've saved with Add to library on a show or movie page, synced across your devices.

  • Saved and Watched switch between your saved titles and what you've finished.
  • Narrow the list by type (Movie or Series) and Genre, and change the Sort.
  • Cloud lists playable files from a connected debrid or cloud account.

If you used Stremio, bring its library and watch history over with Settings ▸ Integrations ▸ Stremio ▸ Import Library. This only reads from Stremio; nothing is written back.

Calendar

Upcoming episodes of shows in your library and Continue Watching, plus movie releases.

  • Filters: Shows, Movies and Sports at the top. Choose each to turn it on or off.
  • Views: List or Month, set in Settings ▸ Layout & Discovery ▸ Calendar View.
  • Month view: move between days with the arrow keys; Return on a day opens its episodes in a panel. Esc closes the panel.
  • Use the arrows beside the month name to go to the previous or next month.
