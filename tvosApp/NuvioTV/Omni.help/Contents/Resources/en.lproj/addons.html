Add-ons

Install, edit, reorder and remove the add-ons that provide catalogs and streams.

Add-ons supply everything you browse and play: catalogs (rows of titles), metadata, subtitles and streams. Omni uses the same add-ons as Stremio and Nuvio.

Install an add-on

  1. Copy the add-on's install link. It ends in manifest.json and usually starts with https:// or stremio://.
  2. Go to Settings ▸ Integrations ▸ Add-ons.
  3. Highlight Add-on URL, press Return, paste the link (⌘V) and press Return.

The add-on is saved to your account, so it appears on your other devices too. Its catalogs wait in Layout & Discovery ▸ Add Catalogs until you add them to Home.

Manage add-ons

  • Order: move an add-on up or down. Streams are listed in add-on order, so put your preferred stream add-on first.
  • Edit: change an add-on's name or URL (for example to change its configuration).
  • Remove: delete an add-on you no longer want.

Debrid services

Many stream add-ons work better with a debrid account. Link Real-Debrid, TorBox or Premiumize in Settings ▸ Integrations ▸ Debrid. See Debrid.

No streams for a title? Check you have at least one stream add-on installed, that it's configured (some need a debrid key), and that Playback ▸ Smart Playback ▸ Cached Only isn't hiding everything.
